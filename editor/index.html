Already comfortable in CS2?

Turn your saved game settings into an autoexec that starts from your own values and bindings.

Where to find your files

Close CS2, then open the account’s cfg folder inside your Steam installation:

userdata/<account>/730/local/cfg

Select these files together from the same account and slot:

  • cs2_user_convars_0_slot0.vcfg
  • cs2_user_keys_0_slot0.vcfg
  • cs2_machine_convars.vcfg (optional)

Only saved values are extracted. Keep custom alias definitions separately. Video settings are not included.

01 / INSTALL
From browser to game.
Place the exported file in game/csgo/cfg inside your CS2 installation. Open the console and run:
exec autoexec.cfg
For startup loading, add +exec autoexec.cfg to Steam’s launch options. CS2 may also load autoexec.cfg automatically.
02 / KEYBOARD
Physical keys. Familiar layouts.
Scancodes refer to key positions. The table shows US QWERTY and French AZERTY labels. Edit a binding’s key to suit your keyboard or mouse.
Mouse4/5 need side buttons. Scancode100 needs an ISO keyboard. Disable or reassign bindings you cannot use.
